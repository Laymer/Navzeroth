## Sources and policies

The [existing source registry](../data/SOURCES.md) lists four composite-related
source IDs and their distinct publisher/attribution/rights records. The owner's
2026-10-12 composite redistribution statement covers that composite only; no new
third-party licence or permission is inferred. A typed sources.json sidecar/full
playable content pack for this standalone Forever dataset remains absent. Do not
relabel its Markdown registry as an installed typed SourceRegistry.

For an installed active ContentRevision, the app separately reports registered and
referenced source counts, unregistered evidence/image source references, declared
conflicts, exact-edition/revision policy matches, observation-status counts for
all16 policy fields, explicit redistribution values and offline true/false values.
Known, unknown, unsupported, restricted and not-applicable remain separate.
Conditional/unrecorded redistribution never authorizes distribution. Recorded
policy claims and confidence categories do not independently prove permissions
or current live behavior. Coverage codes and counters aggregate all records;
there is no hidden row truncation or raw source-URL/personal export forwarding.
