## Implemented and interfaces

Core `SearchControl::checkpoint(SearchPhase)->Result<()>` receives validation,
participant-readiness, edge-assessment, route-expansion, candidate-assessment and
selection phases. `shortest_route_controlled`, `plan_controlled` and
`plan_joint_controlled` take a mutable controller; original signatures delegate
through `NoopSearchControl`. Controller errors propagate, including the existing
OperationCanceled code; an aborted search cannot return partial successful advice.
Nested route searches and joint per-member edge checks share the controller.
Core reads no clock, performs no I/O and retains the existing bounds/tie-breakers.
Individual bounded predicate evaluation and bounded sort/collection operations
complete between checkpoints; no instantaneous preemption is claimed.

Five focused regressions cover each relevant solo phase, joint readiness and
nested routes, late member-edge cancellation, legacy/control route equivalence,
final-success cancellation and input-order determinism. Existing golden planner,
travel and joint tests continue through their unchanged public wrappers.
