## Goal and scope
Pure reminder engine over `ContextRule` and `evaluate_rule` (plan sections 11.1 and
17 M7.1): deterministic priority queue, deduplication, state fingerprint, conservative
cooldown, Session/UntilRulesetChanges/Permanent dismissals, bounded history,
caller-supplied suppression state and a primary-plus-secondary presentation budget.
Out of scope: rule content (M7.2), SQL/persistence (wave 2), UI, any contract or
entity change. No real reminder, threshold or game data is included; every test rule
is synthetic (namespace demo, build SYNTHETIC).

## Implemented
- crates/core/src/reminders.rs (new, plus `pub mod reminders;` in lib.rs).
  - `ReminderEngine::plan(rules, &EvaluationContext, &ReminderInputs, &ReminderState)`
    is pure and read-only. It returns a `ReminderPlan`: `queue` (presentable, ordered),
    `held` (kept back by suppression), `excluded` (rule -> closed `ReminderReason`)
    and `rearm`.
  - `ReminderPlan::select_hud()` gives at most one primary and one secondary item plus
    the remaining queue; the desktop reads `queue` whole.
  - `ReminderState` (serde, validated on deserialize) holds dismissal and
    last-presented records; `dismiss`, `restore`, `prune_inactive` and `commit`.
  - `SuppressionState` holds combat/loading/cutscene as `Observation<bool>`.
- crates/core/tests/reminders.rs: 25 integration tests; 2 unit tests in the module for
  the private fingerprint and cooldown helpers.

## Decisions
- No ADR. Design is recorded here (ADR 0003 forbids numbered ADRs from packages).
- Gating: only `Truth::True` gives advice. `False` gives nothing and re-arms.
  `Unknown` is omitted by default (`UnknownPolicy::Omit`); `Informational` yields a
  `CannotEvaluate` item that carries the evaluation reasons and omits the template.
  An `Err` from `evaluate_rule` (BuildMismatch, step limit) fails the whole plan.
- Ordering: strength (advice before informational), then priority descending (`i16`),
  then `RuleId` ascending. The strength key is a deviation from "priority, then
  RuleId": without it an unknown-input item could take the primary slot over real
  advice. Within one strength the specified order holds. Dedup keeps the best-ranked
  rule per `deduplication_key` before the dismissal/unchanged/cooldown gates, so a
  dismissed or unchanged winner also silences its group.
- Fingerprint: rule, optional snapshot ID, truth and sorted canonical-JSON reasons.
  No hash. Default basis `Snapshot` follows the task (a new snapshot is new state), so
  a still-true rule can repeat once per snapshot after its cooldown; `OutcomeOnly`
  suppresses that. Which is the better default is a review question.
- Cooldown: blocks until elapsed on a trusted clock; untrusted, missing, reversed or
  record-without-trusted-time all block (`CooldownUnverifiable`). Zero cooldown never
  reads the clock. Cooldown applies even when the fingerprint changed.
- Dismissal: keyed by (DataContext, RuleId), so demo and reference never share one.
  Session/Ruleset dismissals need the scope value at dismissal time (else
  InvalidInput) and stay in force unless the new session or revision is known and
  different. History cap 1024; a full history evicts the oldest non-permanent record
  and refuses a new one when only permanent records remain, because evicting a
  permanent dismissal would silently undo the user's choice. The shown-history cap is
  1024 with oldest-first eviction (worst case: one extra presentation).
- Suppression: any fresh known `true` -> `Suppressed` (everything in `held`, not
  dropped). All three fresh known `false` -> `Normal`. Anything else (unknown,
  unsupported, restricted, not applicable, stale, uncertain clock) -> `NonIntrusive`:
  no primary, at most one secondary, which may be advice, so the UI must render that
  slot non-interruptively. No rule is "essential" in wave 1.
- `commit` records only what the caller reports as presented and rejects rule IDs not
  in `queue`; nothing changes on error. A monotonic serial counter, not a clock, orders records.
- Trigger dispatch: an optional caller-supplied trigger list skips rules whose
  `RuleTrigger` is not listed (evaluate_rule leaves dispatch to the caller).

## Limitations
- Suppression, combat, loading and cutscene state are not in CharacterSnapshot; they
  are caller input. Whether the addon can expose them safely is unverified (M0.5).
- No rule has an essential/severity flag, a user "hide" preference model or an
  evidence-gated "disable dependent reminder" capability link; these are not in
  ContextRuleData.
- State is not persisted; no SQL. Reminder content (M7.2) does not exist.
- Presentation-slot semantics (non-intrusive secondary) are a contract with a UI that
  does not exist yet.
- Dismissal does not compare the recorded policy with a later edit of the rule; the
  recorded policy wins.

## Review focus
- Default `FingerprintBasis::Snapshot` versus `OutcomeOnly`.
- The strength-first ordering and the pre-gate dedup.
- Non-intrusive mode showing one advice item as a secondary rather than suppressing
  advice outright.
- A failing rule (Err) aborting the whole plan.
- Eviction rules for dismissal and shown history, and the Deserialize validation of
  `ReminderState`.
