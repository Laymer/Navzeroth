## Delivered behavior

Local Party now offers bounded joint session estimates with explicit goal/utility
unit, shared origin/stopping point, budget, completion/service estimates, hard
exclusions and independent utility for each participant. Nothing fills these
inputs from invented game data. Every selected objective must be authored for the
exact party, be independently ready and contribute positive utility to every
member. Minimum member utility supplies the deterministic greedy score, so one
member cannot dominate another. Declared reward identities remain disjoint.

The service takes a single locked view of party membership, exact profile keys,
current snapshot IDs, active full-context/fixture content revision and freshness.
Changed membership, snapshot IDs or revisions reject stale requests. Missing,
stale or uncertain captures block calculation. Requirements and travel gates are
checked against every participant; unavailable transport cannot become a route.
Authored participant mismatch, missing requirements and unsupported acquisition
inputs remain explicit exclusions. Candidate assessments and edge exclusions are
sorted deterministically. Hard exclusions are bounded and validated against the
active dataset before scoring.

Quest stages need each member's accepted/incomplete/not-turned-in/objective-state
observations. Catch-up remains a separately explained eligibility review above
the planner; this conservative baseline blocks divergent/unknown stage readiness
rather than speculating that a chain becomes unlocked. It does not claim quest
sharing. Existing v1 participant-scoped plan transport is unchanged; joint export
and automatic synchronization are not introduced by this task.

The UI preserves manual drafts on same-party refresh, prunes unavailable catalog
references, invalidates results after edits/refresh, and ignores superseded async
calculations. The detail drawer retains member snapshot/freshness and machine
readiness/decision evidence. Synthetic content remains labelled.
