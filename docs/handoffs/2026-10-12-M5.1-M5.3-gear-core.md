## Decisions
No ADR (ADR 0003 forbids numbered ADRs from packages). Design choices, for the
integration docs PR to promote if wanted:
- Slot pairing is expressed by candidates listing both slots in
  `ItemDefinition.slots`; the layout lists valid slots and the hand pair. A
  candidate naming a slot outside the layout is an error, not ignored.
- The search space is bounded up front: product of (options + 1) per slot must
  not exceed `MAX_CONFIGURATIONS` (4096) and candidates must not exceed 1024;
  otherwise `InvalidInput`. No dominance pruning (skipping it is always valid).
  All subsets are enumerated; assignments with the same item set are one
  configuration, with valid beating uncertain beating rejected.
- `NotApplicable` is a definite answer for `two_handed`, `unique_group` and the
  category (no such property); unknown, unsupported and restricted are not.
- Unknown `two_handed` on a main-hand item with an off-hand partner puts the
  configuration in `uncertain`, never `valid`. Unknown `unique_group` in a
  configuration of two or more items keeps it valid but makes its score
  `Reduced`.
- A definite negative (not proficient, requirement false) dominates unknown
  findings. FutureProgression is only reported when the requirement is made
  solely of `LevelAtLeast` nodes and nothing else is unknown.
- Scores: i64 thousandths, half away from zero, per-item rounding then summing,
  checked arithmetic within the safe-integer range (documented in
  ROLE_PROFILES.md). `top_is_definitive` needs a Complete top and every other
  configuration Complete with a strictly lower total.
- Class comes from the caller (`CharacterProfile.class`), judged by status only.
  Follow-up for a versioned contract PR: class/race/faction on `CharacterSnapshot`
  and a category field on `ItemDefinition`.

## Limitations
- No real role weights, attribute lists or profile ids exist; the four planned
  profiles are undecided and need an owner decision or a registered source.
- Obtainable means equip-eligible only. Acquisition, quest/faction/reputation/
  profession availability beyond the item's own requirement predicate, cost and
  probability are not evaluated (M5.4); there is no Practical ranking.
- A candidate set holds each `ItemKey` once, so two copies of one non-unique
  item cannot both be candidates (a baseline may still repeat a key).
- Class and category are judged by observation status, not freshness.
- The baseline does not validate hand occupancy of the equipped set.
- Configuration enumeration lists every subset (up to 4095 per scenario); large
  catalogs need M5.4-era candidate selection or a validated dominance argument.
- `Certainty::Complete` means no item-level gap, not a complete model.
- Hosted CI, Windows and Lua are unverified; the fixed-point rule is specified
  but only the Rust side exists (parity is M5.6/M5.7).

## Review focus
- `classify` in configuration.rs: the three tiers, NotApplicable vs unavailable,
  and the dedupe of assignments with equal item sets.
- Whether FutureProgression should also cover level gates combined with other
  conditions (currently Ineligible with `requirement_not_met`).
- The search-space bound (product of options + 1) as the only enumeration guard.
- Rounding rule: per-item rounding then summation, and `to_fixed_point` using one
  f64 multiplication.
- `top_is_definitive` strictness (uncertain-validity configurations always block it).
