## Delivered behavior and decisions
`analyze_session(&SessionBuild)` produces a serializable library report retaining
its validated summary (character/session identity, complete data context,
observation statuses and capture metadata, quest/item IDs, original intervals,
gaps and methodology). Reports are per session, never aggregated across
characters, editions/builds or namespaces.

Progression reports the difference of retained known endpoint levels as
Option<i32> (unknown differs from zero), unique observed quest turn-in IDs,
and items present in equipment-change events. These are observations, not
proof of all session progress or item acquisition. XP, planned-versus-actual
comparison and travel milestones remain absent because this event model has
no such observations or estimates.

Timing reports covered monotonic time, observed/estimated/unclassified/gap
totals, per-label quality totals in deterministic order, gap reasons including
zero-width discontinuities, and uncovered time separately. It preserves UTC
uncertainty and all clock flags. Public SessionBuild totals and clock bounds
are checked against the validated summary to reject inconsistent inputs.
No I/O, combat metrics or calibration. No new exchange schemas.
