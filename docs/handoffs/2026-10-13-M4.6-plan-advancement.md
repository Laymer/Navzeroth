## Delivered behavior and review

Loaded Plan revision changes only after successful changed-content replacement or
clear. Identical imports and failed replacements retain in-memory progression.
HUD records fresh positive objective completion by participant and step, skips
completed applicable steps, and reports complete when all are witnessed complete.
Only participant identities in the loaded plan can allocate retained progress.

Alternatives must map to one unambiguous loaded step for that participant. Selection
uses declared order, known-false completion and definite safe readiness; keeps a
ready selected alternative when primary readiness changes. Completed alternatives
are not repeated and never imply primary completion. Missing definitions,
ambiguity, completed/unready/uncertain alternatives carry rejection reasons.
All readiness/completion predicates reject stale inputs.

Optional runtime dependency inputs suspend advice/progression on declared version
or snapshot-reference mismatch; a model flag distinguishes omitted inputs from
supplied checks. Expiry, context and fixture gates remain. Invalidation requests
revalidation and never synthesizes completion. Original schema/fixture acceptance
and independent decoded-plan copies are preserved.
