## Not verified / limitations

- **Professions and enhancements have no data yet.** Wowhead's list pages answer
  scripts with an AWS WAF challenge (HTTP 202); the block is not bypassed.
  Save the 18 pages listed by `import_wowhead_pages.py --list`, import them, then
  run `run_all.py --offline --only gen_professions,gen_enhancements`. The two
  generators are covered only by synthetic-fixture tests.
- Wowhead `/forever` list pages return 403 to scripts; Wowhead data is therefore
  Classic Era, never relabelled.
- 1,118 spells referenced by the client's SkillLineAbility are missing from
  wago's wowf CSVs. Item stat amounts follow the client's stat-budget formula,
  fitted against CMaNGOS (2,409 of 2,823 compared items match exactly); combat
  ratings are omitted and some non-rating stat types are unverified.
- Spawn positions are 1.12 server coordinates placed on the Forever client's map
  layout; near zone borders the zone choice is heuristic. 20 of 27 dungeon
  entrances are `unknown` (the wowf client lacks the classic teleport triggers).
- The registry caps `max_requests` at 1024, fewer than a full Wowhead tooltip run;
  the generators' own limits are the effective bound (WORLD_DATA.md).
- Not run: `pnpm contracts:check` (no exchange schema changed), Windows runs, and
  any live-client comparison. Facts are not registered as admitted source files
  and nothing is activated for planning; the release `DOMAINS` list is unchanged.
- The shared disk filled during the work; `cargo clean` was needed twice.
