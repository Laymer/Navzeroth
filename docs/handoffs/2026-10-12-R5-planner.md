quest acceptance/turn-in eligibility and shortest_route. Explicit exclusions

time and ID tie-breaks; disjoint reward IDs prevent double counting. Retains
eligibility evidence, route exclusions/ranges, feasible alternatives at each
iteration, candidate evaluation count and termination. Includes completion,
service and stopping service in expected-budget feasibility. Rejects intermediate
stateful travel to avoid inventing cooldown/resource resets. Stops at 64 steps.

identifier, normalized integer utilities and all time estimates. This baseline
uses one supplied snapshot evaluator; it does not simulate quest unlocks, supply
party fairness weights, or guarantee range-based worst-case feasibility. Estimated
flag is always true; known/unknown travel ranges remain in returned route hops.
