## Decisions
ADR 0001 records owner choices. Entity aliases wrap editable drafts in Validated;
JSON and constructors share invariants. Compatible extra metadata is ignored.
Integer copper is a decimal string. Source IDs remain opaque strings. Record maps
reject duplicates. JSON input is capped at 1 MiB, text at 4,096 bytes, identifiers
at 256 bytes, maps/declared collections at 1,024 entries. Rule depth/nodes/steps are
32/1,024/1,024 and caller limits may only decrease. These are engineering limits.

Knownness predicates test usability under the selected freshness policy. Stale
known values require opt-in and warnings; uncertain clocks never become fresh.
Field metadata does not silently inherit capture metadata. Equipment slot/rule
constraints reflect explicit supplied facts; no unsupported game rules were invented.
