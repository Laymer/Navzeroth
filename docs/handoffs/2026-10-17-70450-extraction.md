## Finding

`Item` (fileDataID 841626), `ItemSparse` (1572924) and `Map` (1349477) fail with
CascLib `ERROR_FILE_CORRUPT` under strict checksums on 70450 as on 70425, so the
earlier failure was not an update in progress. The pinned CascLib is upstream's
latest (`38a3466`). Task V2.2c diagnoses the cause read-only and makes the
extractor report distinct causes (for example encrypted frames versus checksum
mismatch) instead of one "archive verification failed".

## Update: after V2.2c and V2.2d (main `44df5b8`)

The cause was not corruption: the 106 files have plain leading frames and trailing
frames encrypted with keys the client doesn't have (V2.2c). V2.2d reads and verifies
the plain frames only, records every encrypted range as a gap, never decrypts
(CascLib's built-in keys are disabled at build time) and raised the decoded-cell
bound to 4,000,000 so ItemSparse fits.
