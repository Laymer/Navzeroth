## Change and decision

An installation can stop declaring a locale retained in its durable extraction
choices. Previously the desktop passed that invisible selection to its parent,
enabling extraction with no checked available locale. Restored choices now retain
only exact currently declared locale identities, remove duplicates and sort them.
A visible status explains removed choices and the absence of automatic replacement.
An empty result disables saving and extraction until an available locale is chosen.
No enUS fallback or locale aliasing is introduced; UI prose remains English.

Added eight regression cases: non-English restoration, mixed absent/present locales,
all absent, unknown installed locales, enGB versus enUS, duplicate choices, explicit
reselection, and parent-panel extraction guarding with an unchanged deDE request.
Existing tests cover asynchronous edits, unmount, save/status/acknowledgment errors.
