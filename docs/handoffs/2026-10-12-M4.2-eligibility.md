## Goal and scope
Pure per-participant quest eligibility in `azeroth-core`: acceptance, progress,
completion and turn-in as four separate outcomes, party aggregation, and a
closed reason-code enum. Out of scope: planner (M4.3), explanations rendering
(M4.5), cycle and dangling-reference validation (P9 owns it), CLI/app wiring,
any contract change.

## Implemented
- crates/core/src/eligibility.rs (new) and one `pub mod eligibility;` line in
  crates/core/src/lib.rs.
- Local input types (no contract change): `AccessRequirements` (validated,
  optional class/race/faction allow-lists, non-empty and bounded),
  `ParticipantFacts` (class/race from `CharacterProfile` via `from_profile`,
  faction caller-supplied, keyed by `CharacterId` in `EligibilityInputs.facts`),
  `ExclusiveGroup` (validated, 2..=1024 quests, `taken_when` turned_in or accepted).
- API: `evaluate_participant(inputs, snapshot, party)` and
  `evaluate_party(inputs, party)`. Every predicate goes through
  `evaluation::evaluate()`; each participant gets its own `EvaluationContext`.
- Outcomes: `AcceptanceOutcome` {Eligible, Behind, Blocked, Unknown},
  `ProgressOutcome` {NotStarted, InProgress, AllStagesDone, Unknown},
  `CompletionOutcome`, `TurnInOutcome` {Ready, NotReady, AlreadyTurnedIn, Unknown}.
- Acceptance = eligibility AND prerequisites AND access AND exclusive groups AND
  repeatability, combined with `Truth::and` (False dominates, Unknown stays).
- `PartyOutcome`: JointlyEligible, EligibleForSome {eligible, behind, catch_up},
  AllBehind {catch_up}, Blocked {reasons}, Unknown {missing}.
  Precedence: Blocked, then Unknown, then catch-up outcomes, then joint.
- `QuestEligibilityReason {participant, quest, phase, code}` with a closed
  `QuestEligibilityCode` (`#[serde(tag = "code", rename_all = "snake_case")]`).
  Unknown-side codes come from `EvaluationIssue` (missing record, unavailable
  status, stale, clock uncertain). False-side codes (`prerequisite_unmet`,
  `alternative_prerequisite_unmet`, `level_below_minimum`) come from the
  predicate leaves that evaluated to false, found by re-evaluating sub-nodes
  with `evaluate()`; `Unmet` is the resulting AllOf/OneOf tree used as catch-up.
- Tests: crates/core/tests/eligibility.rs (31 tests) incl. `case_001_*`,
  `case_002_*`, alternative and required chains, level, class/race/faction,
  phase separation, repeatability, unavailable requirement data, stale and
  clock-uncertain, context mismatch, error propagation, shared_progress
  independence, determinism, and a 243-combination property test (18.4).

## Decisions
- No numbered ADR and no contract change. Class/race/faction and exclusive
  choices are local caller-supplied types because `Predicate` and
  `CharacterSnapshot` cannot express them.
- `AllBehind` is a fifth party variant beyond the four named in the package
  brief: when nobody can accept yet but nobody is blocked or unknown, forcing
  that into Blocked would lose the catch-up information. Easy to fold back.
- `Behind` vs `Blocked`: a definite false whose unmet part is only quest
  states (value true) or level is `Behind` (catch-up possible). Negations,
  roles, equipment, locations, preferences, objectives and other participants
  are `Requirement::Other` and block. Access mismatch, a taken exclusive
  choice and an unrepeatable quest already turned in always block.
- Unknown completion semantics gives Unknown even when turned_in is known
  false (conservative, as the brief states). Not-applicable requirement data
  is Unknown, never "no requirement".
- Definition-side observations (`eligibility`, `prerequisites`, `completion`)
  are reference data: only their status is checked, not freshness. Snapshot
  observations follow the evaluator's rules; stage objectives and the access
  facts reuse `observation::freshness()` with the same `StalePolicy`.
- Turned-in implies AllStagesDone for progress (a quest cannot be turned in
  unfinished). Nothing else is inferred; a missing record is never read as
  false or incomplete.
- `shared_progress` is deliberately not read; no sharing claim is produced.
  `JointlyEligible` only means each participant is independently eligible.
- A reason's `participant` is the participant named by the trace issue, which
  can differ from the evaluated one for `participant_state` predicates.

## Limitations
- Contract gap, for a later versioned contract PR: class, race and faction on
  `CharacterSnapshot` (and a faction field anywhere), and class/race/faction
  plus exclusive-choice and collect-before-accept fields on `QuestDefinition`
  or predicate variants. Until then callers supply `AccessRequirements`,
  `ParticipantFacts` and `ExclusiveGroup`. P5 has the same class/race gap.
- Already-accepted quests are not reported as unacceptable; acceptance means
  "could be accepted given requirements and history".
- A Behind participant with other unknown components is still Behind (False
  dominates); those unknowns are listed in `reasons`.
- Catch-up trees are the false leaves only; unknown siblings in an `all` are
  not listed in the tree. `Requirement::Other` is opaque.
- Stage progress needs `QuestProgress.objectives` entries for every stage.
- Sub-node re-evaluation for the unmet tree costs O(nodes x depth) evaluations,
  bounded by the 1024 node / 32 depth limits; each call keeps its own step limit.
- Cycle and dangling-reference checks are P9's. Reasons are not localized or
  rendered (M4.5). Bounded inputs: exclusive groups slice and facts map are
  capped at 1024; allow-lists and groups are capped at 1024.
