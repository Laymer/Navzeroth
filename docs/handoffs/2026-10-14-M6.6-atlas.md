## Behavior and decisions

Dense maps previously rendered every reference pin and list button. Initial
measurements justified bounding both to200 pins per page. Visible wording states
which matching pins the map/list display and that other pins remain on other
pages. Search/type filters still inspect all reference pins. Previous/next pages
and global Home/End/ArrowUp/ArrowDown reach every matching pin; crossing pages
restores button focus. Selection remains explicitly distinct when on another
page, hidden by filters, or removed by refresh. Refresh preserves existing state.
No geography, unlock status, position, dataset identity or source is invented.
Atlas-local styles wrap controls/text and include visible focus at narrow widths
and enlarged text. Shared primary navigation reflow remains coordinator scope.
