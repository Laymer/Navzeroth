Core methodology1 rebuilds canonical retained events under the same 300-second
observed-span / 5-second UTC-tolerance policy as session storage and verifies exact
analytics equality. This prevents fabricated durations or changed terminal events
from masquerading as immutable summary evidence. Cohorts use exact declared
route/stage, level, role and party size. Known retained endpoint levels contradicting
the declared level exclude the sample. A recorded session-start and terminal
matching quest-turn-in, explicit whole-session completed association, and complete
explicitly observed activity intervals are required. Generic observed_span,
unclassified/estimated intervals, gaps (including zero-width discontinuities),
unknown coverage and zero durations are excluded. UTC never supplies duration.

Whole-session association and role/party conditions remain user declarations;
they do not prove route duration. UI labels results conditional, displays the
baseline/source, sample count, descriptive range, exclusions and retained declared
annotations. Median retains extremes; outlier flags mean outside [median/3,3*median].
Blend is floor((5*baseline+n*median)/(5+n)); sparse data therefore cannot immediately
replace the baseline. No probability, learned model or automatic planner update.
Refresh/request edits invalidate reports; stale profile/refresh responses are ignored.
