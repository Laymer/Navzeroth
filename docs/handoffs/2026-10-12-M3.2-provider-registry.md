## Goal and scope
A pure core module for plan 8.3-8.5: a validated `SourceRecord` with the nine
8.3 field groups (unknown kept distinct), a `SourceRegistry` that rejects
duplicate registration and enforces register-before-ingest, an ordinal
precedence function over the existing `Confidence` enum, deterministic conflict
classification that never uses last-writer-wins, and a local origin wrapper for
cross-edition isolation. No SQL, CLI, envelope or UI integration.

- `SourceRecordData`/`SourceRecord` (Validated), enums `RecordClass`,

## Decisions
- No ADR (ADR 0003 forbids numbered ADRs from packages); the design note is the
  slug-named doc above.
- Relabel handling classifies and demotes rather than rejects, so evidence is
  retained: evidence whose origin edition differs from the record's edition and
  that claims more than `cross_edition_unverified` gets effective category
  `cross_edition_unverified` and a `cross_edition_relabel` conflict. Same-edition
  origin must equal the record context exactly; origin and record namespace must
  match (`BuildMismatch`).
- Conflict kinds: `category_downgrade` when every disagreeing record is strictly
  lower than the preferred one, otherwise `value_disagreement`. Payload equality
  ignores which evidence IDs a payload cites.
- Evidence ID reuse with different content is an error; identical is a no-op.
- A source with known edition coverage that excludes the evidence origin edition
  is a `BuildMismatch`; unknown coverage is allowed.
- Candidates and total evidence records are capped at 1024 and rejected, not
  truncated. `EvidenceRecord.conflicts` is not interpreted.
- Conflicts are plain serde records without JsonSchema registration; no schema
  or contract file changed.

## Limitations
- Envelope/schema integration (ReferenceDataset/EvidenceBundle), SQLite
  persistence of conflicts (M3.1 `source_conflicts`), immutable revisions (M3.6),
  CLI wiring and UI exposure of disagreements are deferred to wave 2.
- Relabel detection depends on the declared `origin`; a producer that falsifies the
  origin as well is not detectable in this module.
- Only items and quests are modelled; transform kinds and licence texts are
  free-form `Text`, not interpreted. No real sources or licences are recorded;
  all fixtures use synthetic IDs, build SYNTHETIC.
- The `Redistribution`-unknown-means-not-permitted rule is documented, not
  enforced by any consumer yet.

## Review focus
- The relabel rule (origin edition differs and category above
  `cross_edition_unverified`) and whether demotion rather than rejection is the
  preferred policy.
- The `category_downgrade` versus `value_disagreement` boundary for mixed groups.
- `ingest` re-checks registry membership for previously ingested records, so a
  registry lacking an earlier source fails the whole call.
- Boxing of `RecordPayload` variants (clippy large_enum_variant).
