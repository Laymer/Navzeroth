addon feature through buttons and fields with a tooltip on every control, open it from

development capture only (UNVERIFIED), fix three confirmed open findings, and add a

## Decisions
- No ADR was written or edited here: ADR 0005 is owned by G1 (`docs/g1-reconcile`, not
  merged at this base). Whichever of G1 and G3 merges second should add the entry-point and
  70425 decisions below to it. The decisions are recorded in ADDON_GUI.md and
  BUILD_CAPABILITIES.md.
- Opening is a combat-only gate; build admission is shown as an explained state and each
  action keeps its own gate. The window can therefore open on any build.
- The window has no HUD anchor control (no slash equivalent; dragging and Reset cover it).
- Buttons stay clickable on a non-admitted build and refuse with a recorded reason;
  buttons are disabled only for state preconditions (recorder phase, clock check,
  profile id, quest event, stored export).
- `db_version` stays 1; a 0.9.1 addon drops the three new keys on its next merge.
- TOC title unchanged (`Azeroth Navigator Diagnostics`); registered events unchanged.
