## Changes

- Today and Settings persist bounded explicit local choices and accessibility
  preferences. Pin successful server-owned planning jobs without consuming reports;
  stale or restored advice is clearly historical. Saved export-file choices fill
  Imports inputs only after an explicit click.
- Durable quarantine/revocation blocks activation and safely falls back to a
  validated previously activated eligible revision or clears active content.
- Opted-in local observation review supports CAS decisions, withdrawal tombstones
  and terminal revocation. Accepted-record local export requires current declared
  field/transformation permissions; local acceptance grants no source rights or
  confidence and performs no upload.
- Gear and route acquisition views show exact fixed-point contributions, model
  assumptions, eligibility gaps and bounded traces with readable typed reasons.
- Schema 10 preserves registered 256-byte source identities. Typed backup recovery
  retains settings, queue states and revision counters; restored plan references
  cannot authenticate their own locally guarded publication.
