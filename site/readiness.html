What automation establishes

Hosted checks exercise installation, native IPC, picker cancellation, data preservation, upgrades and downgrade refusal. Synthetic browser and domain tests cover the declared workflows. The executable-line coverage gate requires at least 90% in every Rust and TS/TSX file.

Measurements and observations retain their source, platform and fixture identity.

What still needs evidence

  • The first verified stable live build after launch and its Human class list.
  • Complete, reviewed content and usage permissions for the declared levels 1–20 slice.
  • Candidate-bound live-addon and ordinary-Windows acceptance.
  • Ordinary-Windows performance measurements.
  • Configured and verified stable-release signing.

A finite stable-release scope

Human, levels 1–20; Northshire, Elwynn, Westfall and Redridge; the Deadmines; all launch Human classes; solo and duo. Everything outside that slice needs its own scope and evidence.
